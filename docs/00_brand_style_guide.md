# Brand Style Guide — Minimal Apple-Black Glass

## Visual identity
- Mood: premium, restrained, corporate, chilling.
- Base: near-black (#070A0D), subtle gradients, soft vignettes, low-noise grain.
- UI: thin strokes, rounded corners, layered “glass” panels at low opacity.

## Typography
- Primary: clean sans-serif.
- Weight: headings semi-bold; body regular.
- Effects: restrained shadow/border for readability; avoid neon/glitch spam.

## Motion rules (signature look)
- “Scan pass” when SYSTEM reads context: fast glow sweep + slight blur.
- Alerts: single soft ping; impact moments get a subdued bass hit.
- UI appears decisively; no floaty or playful animation.

## Copy tone
- SYSTEM: polite, calm, procedural.
- HUMAN: grounded, concise, escalating tension.
- Avoid: profanity, slang, politics, real-person claims.

## Composition (9:16 Shorts)
- Safe zone for primary text: center 70% width; avoid bottom 15% (captions).
- Keep 1 primary card at a time.
